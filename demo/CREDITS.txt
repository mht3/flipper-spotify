The Flipper Zero enclosure and controls are based on the GPLv3 exterior STEP
model by Flipper Devices Inc. Eddy Firmware contributors converted and rendered
the model. face.webp is their front view, used here with the same button and
screen coordinates. The repository LICENSE includes the GPLv3 terms.
